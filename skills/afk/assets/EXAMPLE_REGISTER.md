## The cold read-back

Read as the operator would on waking: open the register, answer from it, re-read nothing. What that found, and the measurements are on this session's own log of 8,156 lines.

**It works, on the axis it was built for.** The first row is the one to answer first, and it is first because of what it unblocks rather than when it arose. Every row says what is happening in the operator's absence, and rows 1, 2 and 4 say it specifically enough that a *yes* ratifies work already done instead of re-opening it. Row 3's `idle` carries why idling was right, which is the only form `/decide` §5 permits.

**Four defects, three of them in the pointers.**

*   ⛔ **One row's falsifier had fired and the row was not re-put.** Row 1's `meanwhile` named *a tenth rule arriving* as the condition that would make its own reading false. The tenth arrived **four minutes and three seconds** after the register was written, in the same log, and the row has since survived three further appends still reading `proceeding`. **The repair is in the row above.** This is the clause earning its place: a falsifier written at raise time is worth nothing unless the re-put at each gate actually checks it.
*   ⛔ **Two pointers needed a derivation the operator should not have to do.** One named a threadId where it meant `builds/ide133verify_BUILD_LOG.md`; one named a section of the inheritance without the inheritance's path. Both resolve, which is why this is a defect and not a break — but *a threadId is not an address*.
*   ⛔ **One pointer was unaddressable, and one was absent.** Row 1 originally also cited *the attribution close in this log* — a phrase, inside an 8,156-line append-only file. Row 6 cites no artifact at all. Under this skill's card clause row 6 is not parked; it is a title with a lean.
*   ⛔ **The hand-over carried three of the six rows, and dropped the top three.** Written two minutes after the register, the inheritance's own `🔓 Open claims` block held rows 4, 5 and 6 and reached rows 1, 2 and 3 only through a prose sentence naming the log block. Those are the three highest-value rows, including the one blocking ten owed rules. Since the Authority line makes the log history the moment that file is written, a successor who trusts the inheritance misses them.

**What the read-back could not test.** Nobody has read this register after actually sleeping. The hard-floor ordering is unexercised because no row here is hard-floor — so the clause that stops a thread and sorts it to the top is written and unrun.
